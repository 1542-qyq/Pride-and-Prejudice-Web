--So, which of the painted peacocks is our Mr.Bingley?
--Do you dance, Mr.Darcy?
--Not if I can help it.

--Dancing.
--I am so sorry. How is my sister?
--She's upstairs.
--Would you consider pride a fault or a virtue?
